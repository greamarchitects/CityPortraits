# About

CityPortraits is an open framework for building **data-informed digital twins of cities**, combining geospatial data, historical evidence, and rule-based modeling to reconstruct local urban patterns.
It bridges the gap between **quantitative datasets** and **qualitative urban knowledge**, enabling the generation of interpretable and testable scenarios of building stock evolution.

## Collaboration

CityPortraits is an open and evolving project built through collaboration.  
We bring together spatial data, architectural knowledge, and computational methods — and welcome contributors who want to shape how urban patterns are documented and understood.
